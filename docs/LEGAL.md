# Legal notes

*Not legal advice. Laws on linking and streaming differ by country.*

- **No hosting.** Nexonic stores no video and does not proxy, record or restream. Playlists contain links to streams
  that broadcasters publish themselves.
- **Authorization basis.** A link is published only with a documented basis that the broadcaster offers it free to the
  public ([SOURCE_POLICY.md](SOURCE_POLICY.md)). Public reachability alone is not treated as permission.
- **No circumvention.** No DRM, paywall, authentication or geo-restriction is bypassed. Region-limited streams are
  labelled as such.
- **Trademarks.** Channel names and marks belong to their owners and are used only to identify the channels. No
  logos are distributed unless the owner permits it.
- **Licenses.** Code: MIT ([LICENSE](../LICENSE)). Data and reports: CC0 1.0 ([data/LICENSE](../data/LICENSE)).
  Third-party data: iptv-org/database (Unlicense / CC0) — see [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md). No content from
  Free-TV/IPTV (which has no license) is copied.
- **Takedown.** Rights holders can request removal via the copyright issue form or privately. Removal is one commit;
  see [MAINTENANCE.md](MAINTENANCE.md#fast-removal). Removing a link here does not remove content from the
  broadcaster's servers.
- **Privacy.** No analytics, cookies or personal data collection; GitHub's own logging applies to GitHub Pages.
