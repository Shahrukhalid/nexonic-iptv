# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want Nexonic IPTV to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour
Be kind and patient. Assume good faith. Give and accept constructive feedback. Focus on what is best for users.

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, insults or personal attacks; sexualised language or imagery; publishing others' private information;
posting links to pirated or adult content; spam; any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who
break these rules. Report concerns privately via GitHub's private reporting (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) or to the
maintainer's GitHub profile contact. Reports are handled confidentially.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
